Kariba local guide

Kariba Dam Wall

A 128-metre double-curvature arch dam that holds back Lake Kariba, one of the largest reservoirs in the world. A short trip from town.

Matusadona National Park

On the southern shore of the lake, known for Big Five wildlife, walking and vehicle safaris, and birding.

Tiger fishing and lake cruises

Go after tigerfish on the lake, or take a sunset boat cruise from the nearby harbours.

Kariba Heights viewpoint

A high lookout with wide views of the lake, its islands and the Zambezi escarpment. Best at sunset.

We are in Kariba Town, not Mutare

Some booking sites list our address as “Mutare 143 Upper Third Street, Kariba”. That is incorrect. Tino’s Guest House is in Kariba Town, Mashonaland West Province, Zimbabwe, which is over 600 km from Mutare.

Please confirm directions on WhatsApp before you travel and we will send a pin and driving instructions.
